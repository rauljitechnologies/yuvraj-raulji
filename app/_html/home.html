Frequently Asked

Questions
Answered

Everything you need to know before we begin building your brand's digital future. Can't find your answer?

/ 08  Answered

01

What platforms do you specialise in?

Magento 2 (Adobe Commerce), Shopify & Shopify Plus, WordPress & WooCommerce, and headless/custom Next.js commerce architectures. For cloud, I work primarily on AWS (EC2, RDS, S3, CloudFront) alongside Nginx/Apache server stacks with Redis, Varnish, and Cloudflare.

02

How long does a typical project take?

Timelines depend on scope. A Shopify brand store typically takes 3–5 weeks. A Magento 2 enterprise build ranges from 6–16 weeks. SEO & CRO audits with implementation run 4–8 weeks. Every engagement begins with a clear roadmap and milestone schedule agreed upon upfront.

03

Do you work with international brands?

Yes — I work with brands across India, the UAE, Europe, UK, and North America. All engagements are conducted remotely with structured communication, detailed deliverables, and regular milestone reviews. Time zone differences are managed proactively from IST.

04

What does the engagement process look like?

Every project begins with a strategic consultation call to understand your goals and constraints. A detailed proposal follows with scope, timeline, and investment. Once approved, work proceeds through clear phases — discovery, architecture, development, QA, and launch — with regular check-ins throughout.

05

Do you offer post-launch support?

Yes. Post-launch retainers cover ongoing optimisation, security updates, performance monitoring, SEO iterations, and feature development. Support packages are tailored to each brand's operational needs — from lightweight quarterly reviews to dedicated monthly retainers.

06

Can you optimise an existing store rather than rebuild it?

Absolutely. Many of my most impactful engagements are performance audits, CRO programmes, and platform migrations rather than ground-up builds. I assess your current architecture, identify critical bottlenecks, and implement targeted improvements — often delivering faster ROI than a full rebuild.

07

How is AI integrated into your e-commerce work?

AI is embedded practically: intelligent product recommendations, automated catalogue enrichment, smart semantic search, dynamic pricing logic, and operations automation via n8n + OpenAI workflows. The goal is always measurable efficiency or revenue impact — not technology for its own sake.

08

How do I get started?

Simply reach out via the contact form or email toyuvrajraulji@gmail.com. Describe your project briefly and I'll respond within 24 hours to schedule a no-obligation strategy conversation.

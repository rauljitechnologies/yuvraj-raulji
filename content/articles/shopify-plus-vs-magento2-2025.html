The Question Every Growing E-commerce Brand Asks

After managing migrations in both directions, Magento 2 to Shopify Plus and Shopify to Magento 2, I can tell you: there is no universally "better" platform. The right choice depends on your business model, technical team, and growth roadmap. Here's the honest breakdown.

Total Cost of Ownership (3-Year Estimate)

Shopify Plus

  • Platform fee: $2,300/month (up to $800k GMV): scales with revenue above that
  • Apps: $500–$2,000/month for a typical enterprise app stack
  • Development: lower ongoing cost: most customizations are theme-level
  • Hosting: included
  • 3-year TCO estimate: ₹80L–₹1.5Cr

Magento 2 Open Source (Self-hosted)

  • Platform licence: free (Open Source) or $22,000–$125,000/year (Adobe Commerce)
  • Hosting (AWS): ₹80k–₹2.5L/month depending on scale
  • Extensions: ₹2L–₹8L one-time for a full production stack
  • Development: higher: requires PHP/Magento expertise
  • 3-year TCO estimate: ₹60L–₹2.5Cr

Customization Depth

Magento 2 wins decisively here. It is open source PHP: you can modify any part of the system: pricing logic, checkout flow, catalogue rules, admin workflows, integration architecture. Nothing is off-limits.

Shopify Plus allows significant theme customization and has a powerful app ecosystem, but core checkout logic (outside Checkout Extensions) is locked. If your business requires deeply custom checkout flows, complex pricing rules, or unique catalogue structures, Magento 2 is the only real option.

B2B & Wholesale Features

Magento 2 B2B (Adobe Commerce)

  • Company accounts with hierarchy
  • Shared catalogues with customer-group pricing
  • Negotiable quotes
  • Purchase orders with approval workflows
  • Credit limits

Shopify Plus B2B

  • B2B on Shopify (launched 2022): company profiles, custom pricing, net payment terms
  • Good for simpler wholesale models; less flexible than Magento 2 B2B for complex hierarchies

Winner for complex B2B: Magento 2. For simpler wholesale + D2C combined: Shopify Plus is faster to market.

Performance & Scalability

Shopify Plus handles infrastructure automatically: you never worry about scaling for Black Friday. Magento 2 on AWS requires proper architecture (Varnish, Redis, RDS read replicas, auto-scaling) but gives you complete control over performance tuning.

For most businesses under ₹50Cr GMV, Shopify Plus performance is excellent out of the box. For ultra-high traffic (millions of SKUs, flash sales, complex catalogue) Magento 2 with proper infrastructure can outperform.

When to Choose Shopify Plus

  • D2C brand with standard product catalogue and checkout
  • Team without dedicated PHP/Magento developers
  • Speed to market is the priority
  • International expansion with multi-currency/multi-language via Shopify Markets
  • Social commerce (Instagram, TikTok Shop integrations are seamless)

When to Choose Magento 2

  • Complex B2B, wholesale, or multi-store architecture
  • Deep customization requirements: unique pricing, complex catalogue rules
  • Ownership over code and data (no vendor lock-in)
  • Existing Magento 1/2 ecosystem and team
  • Headless architecture with full control over the GraphQL API
